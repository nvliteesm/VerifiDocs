# UX audit: one first-time check, 25 Nov 2026

Method: a fresh browser at 1440 x 900 (light), the built-in example, one real check with Gemini, a screenshot at every step (`.design/ux-shots/`). Then the first visit on a 375px phone. Measured, not guessed: scan 5.9 s, check 22.5 s, 36 s and 8 clicks from first visit to a copied fixed draft.

The core problem: the product never gives the student a moment that feels good. It finishes its work and then waits for the student to work out what to do. The thing they came for (a fixed sentence) is the last item in a scrolling panel.

## HIGH: the journey breaks

1. **No result moment after the check** (`07-check-done.png`). After 22 s of waiting, nothing is selected, the right panel says "Select a sentence in the draft to see its check", and the outcome is a 12px stats line. The tally sentence is read aloud to screen readers only. The student sees red underlines and an empty panel.
   Fix: open the first issue automatically, and lead with one plain line ("4 of 5 claims need a fix. Start with the first one.") and one primary button.

2. **Two presses to get one result** (`05-scan-done.png`). "Scan my draft" stops halfway; the student must press "Check 4 cited claims". Meanwhile the screen leads with a problem ("1 citation is not in your reference list"), four claims say "Not checked yet", and the stats line reads "1 claim checked, 0 supported, 1 need attention". It looks finished and wrong.
   Fix: when the sources are ready, carry straight on from the scan to the check. Keep the pause only when no source is ready (the reason it exists: UAT 23 Nov, ten PDFs held the scan for 38 s).

3. **Waiting is dead time** (`04-scanning.png`, `06-checking.png`). While scanning, the draft the student just pasted disappears behind "Working…" and an empty white panel. While checking, rows stay "Not checked yet" for 22 s and the only motion is a hairline bar.
   Fix: keep the draft on screen while scanning; mark each row "Checking" as it runs and drop each verdict in as it lands (the requests already finish one by one); show "2 of 4 checked".

4. **The fix is last and below the fold** (`09-first-issue-full.png`). Order in the panel: verdict, meaning, evidence, Why, What to do, then the fix. "Apply fix" needs a scroll inside a scrolling panel inside a scrolling page. Three lines say nearly the same thing ("The source says something different", the Why sentence, "Change the sentence to match the source").
   Fix: Before and After with Apply fix directly under the sentence; one short reason line; evidence below that.

5. **The first visit is a wall of text** (`01-first-visit.png`, `90-phone-first-visit.png`). Three dense privacy paragraphs sit above the product; "Scan my draft" is grey with "Press Got it above first"; "Try an example" is a small secondary button. On a phone the notice is the whole first screen.
   Fix: a one-line notice with the details behind a link, and "Try an example" as a clear first action.

6. **Two scrollbars** (`09-first-issue-full.png`). The panes scroll inside, and the page scrolls too ("Start a new check", "Delete this check" and the pay question sit under the workspace).
   Fix: fit the workspace to the window; move "Start a new check" and "Delete this check" into the top bar; ask the pay question after the draft is copied.

## MEDIUM: friction and tone

7. **Applying a fix does not move on** (`10-fix-applied.png`). The student must press Next each time. Fix: go to the next issue after Apply fix or Ignore, with Undo kept.
8. **No end to the loop.** When the last issue is handled nothing says so, and "Copy fixed draft" is a small button beside "Next issue". Fix: a done state ("All 4 handled") with Copy fixed draft as the primary action.
9. **The tone is red and judging.** Results open on "Contradicted" in red; what is fine ("1 supported") is small. Fix: show progress toward done ("3 left"), not only what is wrong.
10. **The same fact three times.** File and page appear in the list row, the properties row and the evidence header; the citation twice.
11. **Too many equal buttons after the check.** "Copy report", "Check again with my sources", "Edit draft", "Next issue", "Copy fixed draft", "Hide sources", "Hide inspector": none says what to do next. "Copy report" is the primary, but fixing is the job.
12. **The start screen asks for everything at once** (`03-example-loaded.png`). Three columns, two negative status cards ("0 of 1 sources readable"), and the sources twice (left list and right card).
13. **Layout glitch**: with the selected claim scrolled, the last list row is clipped and a stray verdict underline shows through (`10-fix-applied.png`, under "Reference not found").
14. **"Checking" during the scan** (`04-scanning.png`): the eyebrow says Checking while the line says "Finding the claims in your draft".

## LOW: polish

15. "Not checked yet" rows have an empty icon column (`05-scan-done.png`).
16. "Hide sources" and "Hide inspector" show while there is nothing to hide (`04-scanning.png`).
17. On a phone the first visit is 2582px tall before the first useful action.

## After Amendment A (same journey, same day)

Screenshots: `.design/ux-shots/after/`. One real check through the example, as before.

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Presses to get a result | Scan, then Check | Scan (the check follows by itself) |
| Scan and check | 5.9 s + 22.5 s, draft hidden while scanning | 23.5 s, draft on screen, rows say "Checking" |
| After the check | Nothing selected, empty panel, 12px counts | First issue open, "4 to fix, 1 supported." |
| Apply fix | Below the fold, inside two scrollbars | Above the fold; the page does not scroll |
| After Apply fix | Stays on the same claim | Moves on; "Fix applied to C-2. Undo" |
| End of the loop | None | "All 4 handled. Copy your fixed draft to use it." with Copy fixed draft first |
| First visit on a phone | 2582px tall | 1922px tall |
| First visit to copied fixed draft | 36.1 s | 30.8 s, ending in the done state |

Still open: the claim list cuts its last visible row at 40% of the column (ordinary scrolling, but it can read as a glitch); dark mode was not re-screenshotted for the new pieces (they use the same tokens).
