## Bigger ideas (need a decision, not a fix)

These come from the research below. Each is a feature, not a repair, so they are not numbered fixes.

- **A. Draft view with the student's own text.** Show the draft with each claim underlined by verdict colour (and symbol), and open that claim's card on click or tap, the way a writing assistant shows suggestions. Today the claims are cut out of the draft, so the student loses where each sentence sits. Needs each claim's position in the draft; whether the stored claim rows carry character offsets was not checked. Size: medium to large, no new dependency.
- **B. Source page peek.** The page chip ("page 2") opens that page's stored text with the quote highlighted. Hard Rule 4 deletes the PDF after ingestion but keeps page text for 30 days, so this works without breaking it. It shows the page text, not a picture of the page. Size: medium.
- **C. Findings 4 and 15 together** are the smallest version of the research: a tally that filters, and marked numbers in the quote.

Not recommended: three-way citation labels like Scite's supporting, contrasting and mentioning (those describe other papers citing a paper, not a student's sentence against a source); percentages or confidence scores of any kind (R-17: no number without a real source, and the two-model judge does not produce one); a three-panel layout (it fails at 375 px).

## Research (what the findings and ideas lean on)

Search results and preprints, not tested on VerifiDocs. Most are workshop papers or preprints with unreplicated effect sizes.

- Citation chips beside the claim and an evidence side panel that shows titles, dates and previews: [Telerik citation component](https://www.telerik.com/design-system/docs/components/citation/), [SaaS Frame citations pattern](https://www.saasframe.io/patterns/citations-sources).
- A three-panel claim-and-evidence layout where clicking a claim dims the rest: [PaperTrail](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2602.21045).
- Colour-coding evidence as direct support or direct contradiction, each with a short explanation: [Attribution Gradients](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.00361v1).
- Highlighting full phrases got the highest preference in a 208-person study, and design strategy changed trust and ease of checking: [Highlight All the Phrases](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.06846v1).
- Colour-coded cues on quotes lifted trust ratings and source views; showing uncertainty helps people calibrate trust: [Presentation strategies for fact-checked content](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2308.10220); users can trust a checking model even when it is wrong: [Interactive AI design eye-tracking study](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2202.08901); evidence access has strong effects on decisions: [Show me the evidence](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.11387).
- Verdict tallies per category in the interface: [Scite](https://scite.ai/features).
- Underlines coloured by category, with a card per suggestion that is accepted or dismissed, and a document side panel: [Grammarly](https://www.grammarly.com/blog/engineering/how-suggestions-work-grammarly-editor/). The search on Hypothesis-style annotation sidebars returned nothing, so no claim is made about it.
