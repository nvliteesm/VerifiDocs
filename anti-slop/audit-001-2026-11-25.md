Mode: AFTER (chosen by Steven this session). Scope: the new "Search the web" button and card (`WebSources` in `src/components/ResultsView.tsx`, `web.*` and `err.web_*` messages, `webReferenceEntry` and `webInTextCitation` in `src/lib/client/cite.ts`). ## Resolved the same day

Steven chose to fix 1 to 4. Re-checked in the same real-browser run (production build, dev database, real services):

- 1 fixed: the notice now says "Search the web sends them to Tavily, a web search service that may keep them" (and the Indonesian equivalent); a test checks that Gemini, Crossref, OpenAlex and Tavily are all named in both languages. Seen in the browser: the notice names Tavily.
- 2 fixed: `plainText` in the adapter turns the service's markdown into plain text before the model sees a snippet and before the quote check (headings, list markers, bold and simple italics, links, images, code, tables, rules, html; plain text, underscores, a lone asterisk and minus signs are left alone). Seen in the browser: no raw markdown left in the card, and the sentences read cleanly ("Individuals, as always, can use Notion for free forever."). Tests: 6.
- 3 fixed: the last word of a link title and its arrow sit in one non-breaking span, and a trailing period is dropped from the shown title. Seen: link ends "Plan ↗", "Labs ↗", "Enterprise ↗"; no title ends in a period. The paper block has the same arrow pattern and was not changed (outside this audit's scope).
- 4 fixed: both "Possible sources" and "Possible web pages" are `h4` headings (the results page has `h2`, and `h3` for sections); `.found-title` keeps its look. Seen: one `h4` in the web card; focus still moves to it when the answer arrives.
- Contrast, 375 px overflow, tap targets and console were unchanged (same numbers as above), and the test run was deleted afterwards.
- 5 stays open: the Indonesian strings still need a native speaker (now 10 plus the notice sentence).

(n.d.). Notion Pricing Plans: Free, Plus, Business, & Enterprise. Retrieved November 25, 2026, from https://www.notion.com/pricing`.

- R-27 states: loading, empty ("No web page found.") and error (the service's message, button back) are covered by component tests.
